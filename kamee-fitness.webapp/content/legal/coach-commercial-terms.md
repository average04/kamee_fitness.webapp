# Coach commercial terms — draft for review

This proposal is not published and cannot be accepted as the current seller agreement. The final text, version and content hash must be reviewed and published separately before selling is enabled.

## Selling a plan

You choose a supported PHP reference price in the Coaching Hub. Reference prices help configure the store product; members see and pay the price and currency displayed by Apple or Google for their storefront. Saving a price request does not immediately change the live store price. Kamee shows when setup or review is pending, and when each platform's price has been verified. We will not silently replace your requested amount with a different reference amount.

You must own, or have sufficient permission to license, every part of a plan and its attached meals, images and videos. You remain responsible for accurate descriptions, appropriate qualifications and content within your professional scope. A purchase includes the services described at checkout; it does not promise a future chat, personal coaching service or feature that has not launched.

## Proceeds, refunds and adjustments

Your share is 80% of actual net proceeds received for your plan after the store's fees and applicable deductions. Kamee's share is 20% of those net proceeds. A reference price, an order or an estimated amount is not a promise of a particular payout or currency conversion.

Refunds, chargebacks, reversals and later settlement corrections may reduce or reverse the related earnings. A negative balance may carry forward and offset later earnings; the same adjustment must not be deducted twice. Store rules and any rights that apply to the buyer continue to govern the purchase.

## Monthly payout review

Payouts are reviewed by an admin monthly, after the corresponding store reports and funds have been reconciled. An unsettled sale, a refund investigation, missing required payee information or another recorded hold may delay payment. The dashboard must distinguish estimates, settled earnings, holds and amounts actually paid. It must not present a pending payout as sent.

Before a payout, Kamee requests the payee, country, payment and tax information required for the relevant arrangement. Those details are private and are not part of your public coach profile. Specific requirements and payout arrangements must be confirmed before launch; this proposal does not invent a universal tax rate, fee or payment deadline.

## Buyer licence and retained content

For each completed purchase, you grant Kamee a non-exclusive licence to host, reproduce, format, deliver and maintain the purchased plan version and its necessary media for the authorized buyer. This licence continues for existing buyer rights and unresolved or recoverable purchases after you pause sales, remove your public profile or delete your coach account. It does not authorize continuing to promote your removed profile.

A buyer retains the version they purchased and any later approved version they explicitly accept under the available update rules. Changes to your public listing do not silently replace a buyer's plan. Completed workout history remains separate from future access; a refund can end paid access without deleting the buyer's recorded history.

You may stop future sales through the available controls or by contacting Kamee. Stopping sales does not itself erase existing buyer rights, payment evidence or content needed to resolve a pending payment or refund. Illegal or unsafe content and applicable legal obligations may require a separate response.

## Commercial records and account deletion

We retain minimized purchase, consent, refund, settlement and payout evidence as described in the final privacy and retention notices. Seller consent records include the accepted terms version, content hash and acceptance time. Removing a public profile does not remove records still needed for existing buyer rights or unresolved commercial matters.

Account deletion removes or detaches personal app/profile data through the established erasure process. It does not transfer a deleted buyer's pending purchase to a newly created account. Required retained evidence and content remain restricted to authorized access. Unresolved evidence and lawful holds are handled separately from routine deletion schedules.

## Changes and acceptance

These commercial terms are separate from coach onboarding terms. Before selling or requesting a selling-price change, you must explicitly accept the currently published commercial version and its exact body. A later material change requires a new version and acceptance. An old onboarding checkbox or a draft proposal is not commercial consent.
